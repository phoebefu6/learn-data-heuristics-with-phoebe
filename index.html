Learn Data Product Heuristics with Phoebe

There are at least 32 published principle sets that bear on a data product - usability heuristics, dashboard pitfalls, data-mesh principles, FAIR, DataOps, the golden signals, Microsoft's human-AI guidelines - holding roughly 215 individual principles between them. Nobody reviews anything against 215 principles. This course does what Nielsen did in 1994: keeps the few that carry the weight and throws the rest away. Nine sessions assemble one small review instrument, layer by layer, and run it over Tideline, a subscription-analytics product carrying twenty real defects. A well-trained usability reviewer finds five of them.
